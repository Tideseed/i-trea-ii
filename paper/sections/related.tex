\paragraph{Keeping infeasible solutions.} Deb's feasibility rules~\citep{deb2000efficient} made ``feasible beats
infeasible'' the common default in constrained evolutionary optimisation; surveys cover the many
alternatives~\citep{coello2002survey,mezuramontes2011survey}. Several methods keep infeasible solutions on purpose:
ASCHEA steers the share of feasible individuals~\citep{hamida2000aschea}; FI-2Pop keeps a separate infeasible
population~\citep{kimbrough2008fi2pop}; Farmani and Wright single out the infeasible solutions whose objective beats the
best feasible one~\citep{farmani2003selfadaptive}; SMES copies the best infeasible solution into the next
population~\citep{mezuramontes2005smes}; IDEA reserves a fixed share of the population for infeasible
solutions~\citep{singh2011thesis}. I-TREA~\citep{orbay2011itrea} adds three ranks and an interactive decision maker.

\paragraph{Heuristics cooperating with exact solvers.} Sequential hybrids pass solutions one way, for example a
genetic algorithm's first integer solution as the starting incumbent of branch-and-bound~\citep{nieminen2003gamilp}. Two-way exchange of incumbents between a heuristic and a branch-and-bound
solver running in parallel was shown for simulated annealing on MIPLIB 0-1 problems~\citep{nwana2005cooperative} and for
a memetic algorithm beside a branch-and-cut solver on multidimensional knapsack
problems~\citep{puchinger2005cooperating}; a classification of such combinations is in~\citep{puchinger2005combining},
and later examples interleave memetic algorithms with branch-and-bound~\citep{gallardo2007mabnb}. Inside MIP solvers,
combination and mutation heuristics recombine solutions from the solver's pool~\citep{berthold2006primal,hendel2022alns}.
Among exact solvers, racing with shared incumbents is classified in~\citep{ralphs2018parallel} and measured, with
modest gains, in~\citep{carvajal2014diversification,shinano2018fiberscip}, while commercial concurrent
optimisation and some parallel portfolios race without exchanging solutions~\citep{gurobi2026concurrent,
yilmaz2025parbalans}. ReXi runs local-search and large-neighbourhood workers beside SCIP workers over a shared
solution pool~\citep{mexi2026rexi}. Matheuristics are surveyed in~\citep{boschetti2022matheuristics}.

\paragraph{GPU primal heuristics and GPU evolutionary computation.} First-order LP solvers on the
GPU~\citep{applegate2021pdlp,lu2023cupdlp}, GPU bound propagation~\citep{sofranac2022domainprop} and LP-free local
search~\citep{luteberget2023feasibility,lin2024localmip} are the building blocks of current GPU MIP heuristics. NVIDIA
cuOpt combines them into a GPU primal-heuristic stack~\citep{corduk2025gpuprimal} and, in its source, a GPU population
with recombination that keeps infeasible members and protects the best feasible one, running beside cuOpt's own CPU
branch-and-bound~\citep{corduk2025evoframework,nvidia2026cuoptsrc}. CHAP pools feasible and infeasible candidates of GPU
and CPU heuristics~\citep{tjusila2026chap}; fix-and-propagate guided by GPU LP solutions has been applied to
unit-commitment-scale models~\citep{kempke2025fixprop}; other recent work explores many subproblems or penalty-tempered chains in
parallel on the GPU~\citep{pauphilet2026pangea,sim2026ptilp}. General GPU frameworks for evolutionary computation
exist~\citep{huang2024evox,toklu2023evotorch,lange2022evosax}.

\paragraph{Position.} We use these methods and claim no novelty for any of them. The combination studied here --- a
GPU population with a capped, incumbent-relative share of infeasible solutions, coupled with an external general MIP
solver through two-way exchange --- was not found in our search, which is a negative search result, not a priority
claim. Works we could not read legally are listed separately in Appendix~\ref{app:paywalled}.
