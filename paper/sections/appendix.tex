\section{Paywalled research}\label{app:paywalled}
This appendix is the only place in the paper that names work we could not read. The following works appear relevant
but are behind publisher paywalls, and we found no legal free copy (preprint, accepted manuscript, repository or thesis
version). \emph{We have not read them in full}; what is said about them is limited to their abstracts, metadata and the
open works that describe them. They have no entries in the bibliography; each is identified by its DOI.
Stochastic ranking for constrained evolutionary optimisation (Runarsson and Yao, 2000, doi:10.1109/4235.873238), an
optional parent comparator in our code; the infeasibility-driven evolutionary algorithm IDEA in its chapter and
conference versions (Ray et al., 2009, doi:10.1007/978-3-642-00619-7\_7; Singh et al., 2008,
doi:10.1007/978-3-540-89378-3\_11), read through the open thesis~\citep{singh2011thesis}; the $\varepsilon$-constrained
method (Takahama and Sakai, 2006, doi:10.1109/CEC.2006.1688283); an evolutionary algorithm for polishing MIP solutions
(Rothberg, 2007, doi:10.1287/ijoc.1060.0189) and relaxation-induced neighbourhoods (Danna, Rothberg and Le Pape, 2005,
doi:10.1007/s10107-004-0518-7), read through~\citep{berthold2006primal}; a hybrid genetic algorithm and branch-and-bound
approach (French, Robinson and Wilson, 2001, doi:10.1023/A:1011921025322); constraint-based local search in CP-SAT
(Davies, Didier and Perron, 2024, doi:10.1007/978-3-031-60597-0\_16); and the account of performance variability in MIP
(Lodi and Tramontani, 2013, doi:10.1287/educ.2013.0112). \pending{Complete from the vault note ``Paywalled research''
before submission.}
