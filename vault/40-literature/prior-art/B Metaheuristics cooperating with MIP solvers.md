# Prior art B — metaheuristics cooperating with MIP solvers

Sweep date 2026-10-05. Question asked of each work: does a heuristic (population or not) run beside or inside an exact
MIP / branch-and-bound solver, and which information crosses between them, in which direction, and when?

**Rank names** follow the vault ([[D-004 Mapping I-TREA (2011) to I-TREA II]]): **Rank 0 = infeasible good** (the
brief's "R2"), **Rank 1 = feasible good**, protected (the brief's "R1"), **Rank 2 = rejects** (the brief's "R3").

**Sources and rules** as in [[A Constraint handling with infeasible solutions]]: legal copies only; paywalled-only works
are cited only in [[Paywalled research]]. Verification levels: *Full text read*, *Abstract / landing read*, *Metadata
only*.

## 2. What this means for I-TREA II

The cooperation patterns in this sweep, by what crosses between the parties:

| pattern | works | what crosses |
|---|---|---|
| race without exchange | `gurobi2026concurrent`, `yilmaz2025parbalans` (gaps reported only); optopt's SCIP‖cuOpt race | nothing (or reporting only) |
| race among exact solvers with exchange | `shinano2018fiberscip` (incumbent broadcast, measured gain), `carvajal2014diversification` (solutions, bounds, cuts; gain "usually mild"), `ralphs2018parallel` Alg. 3 (upper bounds) | incumbents, bounds |
| heuristic workers beside B&B workers, shared pool | `mexi2026rexi` (LS + LNS workers, crossover/mutation over the pool), `perron2023cpsat` | solutions both ways, bounds |
| metaheuristic and exact solver in parallel, two-way | `puchinger2005cooperating` (MA + CPLEX B&C, MKP; duals too), `nwana2005cooperative` (SA + B&B, MIPLIB 0-1; branching information too), TECHS (via `puchinger2005combining`), Gallardo et al. 2007 (interleaved, [[E Novelty check 2026-10-05]]) | incumbents both ways, plus duals / branching hints |
| EA operators inside B&B | `rothberg2007polishing`, `berthold2006primal`, `hendel2022alns` | pool solutions as parents |
| sequential seeding | `french2001hybrid`, Nieminen et al. 2003 ([[E Novelty check 2026-10-05]]) | one direction, once |

So two-way incumbent exchange between a population heuristic and an exact solver is **established** (Puchinger–Raidl–
Gruber 2005 is the closest design: a received solution replaces the population's worst member, as I-TREA II's injected
solutions enter the candidate set), and so is incumbent broadcast among racing solvers, with measured but modest gains
(FiberSCIP 1.05×/1.17× geometric-mean time; Carvajal "usually mild"). What I-TREA II changes relative to these is the
population's location (GPU), the partner (a general MIP solver — SCIP or HiGHS — rather than a problem-specific B&B), and
the population's structure (a capped incumbent-relative infeasible rank and a protected feasible share). None of the
cooperative designs above keeps infeasible solutions on purpose.

Design consequences already visible:
- Injected EA solutions reach SCIP's crossover/mutation/ALNS neighbourhoods only through SCIP's solution storage; that
  this helps is an inference to be measured (posts accepted vs used), not a cited fact.
- ReXi admits only improving solutions to its pool; I-TREA II's "very good, not best" injection (`inject_band`) has no
  direct counterpart there, so the band ablation in D-002 is informative.
- ReXi (and SCIP's concurrent mode) is a stronger CPU control than SCIP alone; see [[Prior art]] §5.
