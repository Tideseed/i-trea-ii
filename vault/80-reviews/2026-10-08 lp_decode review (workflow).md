## Notes for the fixes
- Several findings are one issue seen from three lenses: two decoded batches can join one candidate set (plan_memory budgets N, concatenation can reach 7 parts) and copies usually enter two generations late — fix together: poll just before `_with_pending`, at most one decode batch per candidate set.
- The multi-MB pipe transfer on ca (9.4 MB request, 5.1 MB reply): one refuter showed it costs ≈ 0.1–0.25 % of wall on ca (≈ 22 offers per run) and the design deferred shared memory to a measured trigger (M1); the other confirmed the blocking. Decision for tomorrow: cheap mitigation (int8 payload when the integer bounds fit: ca is all binary, 4× smaller) + measure in the smoke run; shared memory only if M1's 50 ms per-call gate is at risk.
- Measurement fidelity for D-017's M0–M4: per-kind (F/I) entered/incumbent/post counters, priming vs warm time-limit hits, the worker's own ready time, and a sync before the host timer so M1 does not charge the survival's GPU tail to the decoder.
- Tests: make the no-gain filter, the budget hand-off and finish()'s drain non-vacuous; de-flake the rebasing and basis-restore tests; run the GPU cold-cache test before any window.
- Pre-existing: `test_runner_env_and_pinning_in_a_fresh_process` fails whenever the test process's affinity excludes RUNNER_CORES (true at the base commit too) — skip with a reason in that case.
