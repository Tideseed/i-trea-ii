## 10:1x What GAMS's data says about v1's external target (analysis, not a result of ours)
From `primalintegral.csv` (one run per instance and solver; CPU solvers on an Intel Core Ultra 9 285K):
- cuOpt-GB10's hard part (P ≥ 0.1 or no solution, D-019's definition): **75 of 233** (7 without a solution); mean P there
  0.601 (SCIP 10 0.968, SCIPConc-11 0.679, ReXi 0.547, HiGHS 0.691, the commercial mean 0.281).
- A **perfect race** of cuOpt-GB10 and single-thread SCIP 10 (per-instance minimum, no resource sharing) gives a geo-mean
  of 0.0304 over the 233 (−23 %) and a hard-part mean of 0.526 (−12.5 %). D-019's v1 target (hard part −25 %, geo-mean
  −10 %) therefore needs the coupling to add about as much again beyond a race that pays nothing for sharing the box.
- With SCIPConc-11 instead of SCIP 10 the perfect race reaches 0.0187: concurrent SCIP complements cuOpt far more than a
  single SCIP thread — an input for the full-box A9 layout (#30): how many threads SCIP gets against cuOpt's.
- 13 of our 17 development / class-probe instances are among the 233 (the external result is reported with and without
  them, D-019). (Corrected to 15 at 12:1x.)
